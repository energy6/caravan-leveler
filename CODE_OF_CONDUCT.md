# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and welcoming
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints.
- Accept correction gracefully and help others learn.
- Protect private information and avoid publishing sensitive data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, discussions, and other
spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the
repository owner's GitHub contact options. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible. Do not open a public issue containing
personal or sensitive information.
